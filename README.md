# LE/DIGT 1302 Team Project Overview

## DormScope

https://hyper360.github.io/DormScope/

Find a suitable dorm by seeing the past dorm experiences of other users and filter dorms based on your requirements as a student. Made for University/College Students, specifically those who are looking for on-campus or nearby off-campus housing.

Students will have an easy way to:

- Find available dorms/student housing options
- Compare housing based on location, price, campus, and distance
- View photos and basic details about each dorm
- Read ratings from other students
- Submit their own dorm experience through a rating form

## Planned Features and nice-to-haves
- Finding college dorms
- Search/Sort filters (by campus, on/off campus, proximity, price)
- Pictures of dorms
- Rating experience
- Survey to filter dorm needs
  - Own bathroom vs fine with floor vs sharing
  - Bunk bed
  - Kitchenette
  - Route to campus etc.
- Local storage rating + reviews
- Survey, sort + filtering

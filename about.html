About Dorm Scope

Dorm Scope is a student housing project built to help York University students discover and compare dormitory-style options.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dorm Scope?

It is a web application to help students compare dorms and housing options.

How does it help students?

By offering draft listings, key housing details, and a clear site structure for housing discovery.

Why was it created?

To make student housing search easier and more organized for first-year and returning students.

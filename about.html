About Us

Dorm Scope is a project created by a group of students who wanted to make it easier for their peers to find the perfect dormitory. We understand the challenges of finding suitable housing, and we are committed to providing a user-friendly platform that helps students make informed decisions about their living arrangements.

FAQ

What is Dorm Scope?

Dorm Scope is a platform designed to help students compare dormitory options and make informed decisions about where to live.

Who is Dorm Scope for?

Dorm Scope is for students who are looking for clear, helpful information about dorms and student housing.

Why was Dorm Scope created?

Dorm Scope was created to make the dorm search process easier, less stressful, and more organized for students.

How does Dorm Scope help students?

Dorm Scope helps students by providing dorm-related information in one place, making it easier to compare options and choose housing that fits their needs.
